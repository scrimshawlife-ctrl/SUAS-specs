## Terms
- **Shut Up and Serve (SUAS)**: the product defined by this specification stack.
- **SUAS-specs**: this repository, which is the canonical specification authority.
- **Canonical loop**: `SIGNAL -> NEED -> CONSENT -> COORDINATION -> FULFILLMENT -> FOLLOW-UP -> SETTLEMENT`.
- **CheckIn**: a structured status update submitted by a veteran or authorized helper.
- **SupportSignal**: deterministic level assignment of `GREEN`, `YELLOW`, `ORANGE`, or `RED`.
- **SupportCase**: the coordination container around a veteran for a bounded support episode.
- **ServiceRequest**: a single actionable need within a support case.
- **Referral**: a handoff or recommendation to a resource or partner.
- **Assignment**: a linkage of responsibility between a service request and a responder.
- **Fulfillment**: evidence that a request was acted on or delivered.
- **FollowUp**: a post-fulfillment touchpoint or monitoring action.
- **Settlement**: the closure artifact for operational completion.
- **Trusted Circle**: explicit, consent-backed support contacts for a veteran.
- **Responder**: a human actor carrying out support work.
- **Coordinator**: a human actor managing case flow and assignments.
- **Peer Supporter**: a role participating in peer support under granted scope.
- **Admin**: a governance role for configuration and audit review.
- **PARTNER_ORG_001**: placeholder identifier for an unverified partner organization.
- **Santa Clara County pilot**: the initial geographic and operational pilot scope.
- **OBSERVED**: directly recorded or verified fact.
- **INFERRED**: deterministic conclusion from observed facts.
- **SPECULATIVE**: assumption or future-oriented idea needing validation.
- **NOT_COMPUTABLE**: value or claim that cannot be determined from available information.
- **DECISION_PENDING**: unresolved decision requiring ADR review.
- **HIPAA_APPLICABILITY**: the still-unresolved assessment of whether and how HIPAA applies.
- **Implementation authority**: permission state for implementation to rely on settled specs.
- **Pilot readiness**: readiness state for real pilot operation.
