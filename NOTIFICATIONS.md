# NOTIFICATIONS.md — Notification records and policies (SUAS v0.1)

**Related:** [CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md), [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md), [EVENT_MODEL.md](EVENT_MODEL.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)

**Actors:** System (enqueue/send), Veteran / Responder / Trusted Contact (recipients), SUAS System Administrator (templates).

---

## 1. Purpose

Deliver EMAIL, SMS, and IN_APP messages that are **generated by events and policies**, not by template-embedded safety logic.

---

## 2. Channels

| Channel | MVP | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `EMAIL` | Yes | Provider D-004 `DECISION_PENDING` |
| `SMS` | Yes | Provider D-003 `DECISION_PENDING`; unavailable until provider exists |
| `IN_APP` | Yes | No external provider |
| `PUSH` | `FUTURE` | Reserved |

---

## 3. Recorded fields (required)

Every Notification records:

- recipient (`recipient_user_id` and/or address)
- `reason`
- `channel`
- `consent_basis` (Consent Grant id or documented system basis)
- `template_version`
- `sent_at` (when sent)
- `delivery_status` (`QUEUED`|`SENT`|`FAILED`|`DELIVERED`|`BOUNCED`|`UNDELIVERABLE`)

---

## 4. Consent and preferences

1. Evaluate Consent Grant at enqueue **and** immediately before send.
2. If the grant is revoked, cancel the send; record status; do not send.
3. `NotificationPreference` selects channel only when a grant/basis exists. Preferences cannot authorize a send.
4. Trusted-contact alerts: [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md), [TRUSTED_CIRCLE.md](TRUSTED_CIRCLE.md).

---

## 5. Retry

- Failed sends retry with bounded attempts (constant documented; recommended 3 — `INFERRED`).
- Each attempt is recorded (same logical notification with attempt count, or child rows — pick one in implementation and keep audit).
- Exhausted retries: `UNDELIVERABLE`; ops alert ([OPERATIONS.md](OPERATIONS.md)).
- Safety-critical **decisions** are not retried inside templates; red-state decision happens before enqueue.

---

## 6. Generation rule

**Events and policies generate notifications.** Templates render copy. Templates must not:

- decide signal level
- decide whether to alert a Trusted Contact
- decide case closure
- call emergency services

Template copy for red-state resources is D-012 `DECISION_PENDING`.

---

## 7. Events that commonly enqueue (non-exhaustive)

`CHECKIN_COMPLETED` (veteran receipt), `SUPPORT_SIGNAL_CHANGED` (consented recipients), `CASE_ASSIGNED`, `SERVICE_REQUEST_ASSIGNED`, `SERVICE_ACCEPTED`, `SERVICE_FULFILLED`, `FOLLOWUP_DUE`, `TRUSTED_CONTACT_INVITED`, `CONSENT_GRANTED` / `CONSENT_REVOKED` (veteran receipt), `TRUSTED_CONTACT_ALERTED` (the alert itself).

---

## 8. Non-goals

- Safety-critical logic in templates
- Marketing mail
- PUSH in MVP
- Logging full message bodies to application logs ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))

---

## 9. Testability

Critical suite: **notification consent**.

- Revoke between enqueue and send → not sent.
- Preference-off with grant still allows IN_APP if policy says so; SMS/EMAIL honor preference.
- Missing `consent_basis` rejected.
