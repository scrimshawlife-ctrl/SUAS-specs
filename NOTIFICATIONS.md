**Related:** [CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md), [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md), [EVENT_MODEL.md](EVENT_MODEL.md), [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md), [DATA_MODEL.md](DATA_MODEL.md), [RESILIENCE.md](RESILIENCE.md), [SCALING.md](SCALING.md)

**Actors:** System (enqueue/send), Veteran / Responder / Trusted Contact (recipients), SUAS System Administrator (templates/operations).

---

## 1. Purpose

Deliver EMAIL, SMS, and IN_APP notifications as durable, consent-aware logical sends generated by events/policies. Templates render copy; they do not contain safety-critical decisions.

---

## 2. Channels

| Channel | MVP | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `EMAIL` | Yes | provider D-004 when configured |
| `SMS` | Yes | provider D-003 when configured |
| `IN_APP` | Yes | internal |
| `PUSH` | `FUTURE` | reserved |

Unavailable external provider/channel must be reported unavailable; do not fake delivery.

---

## 3. Notification identity

One Notification row represents **one logical send intent**.

Required logical fields:
- `notification_id`;
- tenant scope;
- recipient user and/or destination address/reference;
- `reason` / notification policy key;
- channel;
- `consent_basis` or documented system basis;
- `template_version`;
- optional subject reference `subject_type` + `subject_id` (0.1.4) linking the send to the workflow entity it was sent for, where `subject_type ∈ {SupportCase, ServiceRequest, Referral}` (the canonical entity-type names); additive and nullable, and required for MVP `RESPONDER_NOTIFIED` to be truthfully reachable ([MVP_REFERENCE.md](MVP_REFERENCE.md) §7.2, [DATA_MODEL.md](DATA_MODEL.md) §9);
- logical-send `dedupe_key`/equivalent deterministic identity where the generating policy can be delivered more than once;
- `delivery_status`;
- `attempt_count`, `last_attempt_at`, `sent_at` as applicable;
- timestamps.

Duplicate delivery of the generating Domain Event/job must resolve to the same logical Notification when policy semantics say it is the same send. Do not create multiple messages because a queue delivered twice.

A deliberate later reminder/escalation is a new logical Notification with a new identity.

---

## 4. Consent / preference evaluation

1. Evaluate applicable consent/system basis when creating the logical send.
2. Re-evaluate immediately before each external send attempt.
3. Revoked/expired basis cancels future send attempts and records the cancellation/deny.
4. Preferences choose an allowed channel; preferences never grant consent.
5. Provider/channel delivery status cannot authorize a new disclosure or send.

Trusted-contact alerts remain governed by [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) and [TRUSTED_CIRCLE.md](TRUSTED_CIRCLE.md).

---

## 5. Durable send execution

External notification sends are production-critical durable jobs.

Rules:
- work survives app/worker restart;
- duplicate job delivery is safe;
- each actual provider send attempt is recorded as immutable Audit Event history;
- retry is bounded/backoff-aware and respects provider rate limits;
- exhausted/terminal delivery failure becomes `UNDELIVERABLE` or the appropriate canonical delivery state and is visible to operations;
- unknown/ambiguous provider acknowledgement follows adapter reconciliation policy where supported rather than blind duplicate send;
- exact queue/provider products remain decisions.

No child `notification_attempts` domain table is required; immutable Audit Events remain the attempt-history authority unless a later accepted spec changes this.

---

## 6. Delivery status

Canonical logical delivery statuses:

`QUEUED` → `SENT` | `FAILED` → `DELIVERED` | `BOUNCED` | `UNDELIVERABLE`

Provider-specific statuses map inside adapters.

Rules:
- `SENT` means SUAS/provider accepted the send attempt according to adapter semantics; it does not imply delivery;
- delivery webhooks may move canonical delivery status after authentication/deduplication;
- duplicate/out-of-order webhook events must not regress a terminal truthful state without explicit reconciliation logic;
- webhook receipt does not enqueue another message automatically.

---

## 7. Generation rule

Events and explicit product policies generate Notification intents. Templates must not:

- determine Support Signal level;
- decide whether consent exists;
- decide whether to alert a Trusted Contact;
- decide Case closure;
- invoke emergency services;
- decide provider eligibility/qualification.

D-012 controls approved red-state safety copy.

---

## 8. Common generating facts

Examples include `CHECKIN_COMPLETED`, `SUPPORT_SIGNAL_CHANGED`, `CASE_ASSIGNED`, `SERVICE_REQUEST_ASSIGNED`, `SERVICE_ACCEPTED`, `SERVICE_FULFILLED`, `FOLLOWUP_DUE`, `TRUSTED_CONTACT_INVITED`, consent receipts, and accepted alert policies.

Event existence alone does not mean every possible recipient gets a notification; policy + authorization/basis determines the logical send.

---

## 9. Separation from Follow-Up retries

Notification `attempt_count` measures delivery attempts for one logical message.

It does **not** increment `FollowUp.coordination_attempt_count`. A business coordination attempt is recorded only when the Follow-Up workflow actually performs/records the coordination action.

Queue redelivery is neither a notification attempt nor a coordination attempt unless it causes an authenticated provider send/business action that is actually attempted.

---

## 10. Security / privacy

- Do not log full message bodies in ordinary application logs.
- Keep provider credentials outside domain records.
- Webhooks are authenticated/replay-protected according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Destination/address data is sensitive and tenant-scoped.
- Notification payloads use minimum necessary information for the reason/purpose.

---

## 11. Non-goals

- marketing mail;
- PUSH in MVP;
- template-embedded safety logic;
- process-local volatile production send queues;
- treating queue redelivery as permission to duplicate a message;
- provider-specific delivery statuses leaking into product contracts.

---

## 12. Testability

Required tests include:
- revoke between enqueue and send → not sent;
- duplicate generating event/job creates one logical Notification when dedupe semantics match;
- duplicate worker delivery does not duplicate external send effect beyond provider/idempotency guarantees;
- attempt history appends immutable Audit Events;
- no `notification_attempts` child table under current contract;
- same provider webhook delivered twice is deduped;
- out-of-order delivery callbacks do not silently regress truthful terminal state;
- exhausted retries become operationally visible;
- provider outage preserves Notification state and does not corrupt parent workflow;
- notification retries never increment Follow-Up coordination-attempt count.
